# Credits & Takedown

[English](#english) · [中文](#中文)

---

## English

Every video and prompt belongs to its creator. Each entry credits the author and links back to their original post, which remains the canonical source.

- Prompt text is included only when the creator publicly shared it (in their post or a public reply). Entries based on the creator's public description rather than a verbatim prompt are marked `prompt_partial` and say so in the file.
- View/like counts, where shown, are point-in-time snapshots of public numbers.
- The repository's own content (list structure, scripts, this document) is MIT-licensed. The MIT license does **not** cover the creators' videos or prompts — those remain the property of their authors.

### Takedown

If your work appears here and you want it changed or removed:

1. Open an issue with the [takedown template](../../issues/new?template=takedown-request.yml), or contact the maintainer via their GitHub profile.
2. Say who you are and which entry it concerns. No justification needed.

**We remove or change the entry within 72 hours.**

## 中文

每个视频与 prompt 的版权归创作者所有。每个条目都署名作者并链接回原始帖子,原帖是唯一权威来源。

- 仅当创作者公开发布过 prompt(在帖子或公开回复中)时才收录;基于作者公开描述而非原始 prompt 的条目会标记 `prompt_partial` 并在文件中注明。
- 播放/点赞数(如展示)为公开数据的某时点快照。
- 仓库自有内容(列表结构、脚本、本文档)采用 MIT 协议;MIT 协议**不覆盖**创作者的视频与 prompt——这些归作者所有。

### 下架

如果你的作品被收录,希望修改或移除:

1. 用[下架模板](../../issues/new?template=takedown-request.yml)开 issue,或通过 GitHub 主页联系维护者。
2. 说明身份和涉及的条目即可,无需任何理由。

**72 小时内处理完毕。**
